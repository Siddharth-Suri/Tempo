# Tempo

Tempo is a chrome extension, that tracks time of the websites you visit. It is written is plain vanilla JS. The data is represented in a clean manner with an optional option for bar graphs to help calculate your averages over the past week. 
It also gives you detailed analysis, 2 different themes to choose from and an automatic inactivity detector that pauses counting in case you are afk and don't use your mouse and keyboard. 

## Features

- Tracks time on the active site, only while the Chrome window is focused
- Pauses when you're idle (no mouse or keyboard use) for 2–5 minutes, configurable
- Popup with today's total, a weekly chart and your top 3 sites
- Insights page with weekly and monthly averages, compared against today
- Keeps 30 days of history and clears older data automatically
- Grey and beige themes, compact mode, and an option to hide site icons
- All data stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Requirements 
Requires Chrome 130 or later.
